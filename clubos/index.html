01 / THE REALITY

A familiar kind of chaos

Big ideas deserve
better organisation.

A student club is a small universe. People, events, deadlines, last-minute changes, endless follow-ups. The exciting part is making things happen — not hunting for that one message from three weeks ago.

03“Wait, what's the latest?”

Updates live in too many places, and people miss the memo.
